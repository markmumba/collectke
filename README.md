# CollectKE Landing Page

The landing page and marketing website for CollectKE, an invoice-to-cash workspace being built for Kenyan businesses.

CollectKE is intended to help suppliers, contractors, and service businesses connect the work they deliver with the money they collect. It brings sales documents, payment records, and customer follow-up into one clear view.

The app is designed to help teams:

- Manage quotations, customer LPOs, delivery notes, and invoices in one connected workflow.
- Reconcile bank and M-Pesa payment records with invoices, including partial payments.
- See outstanding balances, overdue invoices, and customer account statements.
- Review, schedule, and pause payment reminders while keeping customer relationships in mind.
- Give owners, finance, and operations teams a shared picture of what is owed and what needs attention.

CollectKE is in development. The website introduces the planned product, explains its features and proposed pricing, and lets businesses express interest in early access.
